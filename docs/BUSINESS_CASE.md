# Business case

## Problem and wedge

A contractor needs to check that a worker's ticket came from the right issuer, remains current, and has not been withdrawn. Paper tickets, screenshots, and forwarded PDFs do not themselves provide an independent live status check. xCertz focuses on trade-ticket issuance, portable proof, employer verification, and expiry/revocation.

## Buyer and user

Training providers and trade-ticket issuers are the proposed paying customers. Workers carry the proof between contractors. Employers verify it without accounts or private worker records. Start with one qualification and one authorized issuer; validate employer acceptance before widening distribution.

## Business hypothesis (not validated revenue)

Offer issuers an annual subscription or per-issued-ticket pricing, with employer verification and worker sharing free. Pricing is not set, and no xCertz paying customers, partnerships, adoption metrics, or market-size estimates are claimed in this submission.

## Go-to-market

Recruit one training provider and two contractors for a bounded credential-verification trial. Measure time to verify, percentage successfully verified, renewal/expiry checks, revocation propagation, and administrative work saved. Obtain explicit employer feedback on issuer trust and worker privacy. Test willingness to pay with the issuer before making a revenue forecast.

## Why Solana

An issuer-signed public record can be checked across organizations without requiring access to the issuing institution's private system. Solana supplies transaction authentication and append-only history; Memo keeps the hackathon implementation small. The prototype does not require workers to buy a token or disclose personal records.

## Founder fit

Derek Chok brings roughly 20 years in welding, pile driving, and ironwork. That field experience grounds the skilled-trades workflow. This is a pre-revenue xCertz prototype; Torch/Jack activity should not be represented as xCertz traction.

## Differentiation and limits

The wedge is a concrete trade-ticket lifecycle, not a generic identity wallet. The current build proves wallet signing and current state; issuer accreditation, legal acceptance of credentials, and proof of subject-wallet control remain outside the MVP. Public RPC history and permanently public metadata are deployment constraints to validate before production.
